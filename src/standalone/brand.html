Brand system · v1.01960labs / identity

We engineer custom solutions to complex problems.

1960 Labs builds custom software, automation and AI for businesses whose processes are slow, manual or error-prone. The brand should feel serious, precise and built to last: an engineering lab, not a creative studio.

01

Precise

Every shape sits on a grid. Every cut is 45°. Every claim has a number.

02

Calm

White space and ink do the work. Blue appears once per view, where the eye should land.

03

Built to last

Solid blocks, heavy type, no trends. It should look right in ten years.

04

Plain-spoken

Problems and outcomes in short sentences. No hype, no robots, no magic.

01 · Logo

Sixty, in two solid blocks.

The mark is the 60 of 1960 drawn as two heavy blocks: the 6 open at the top right, the 0 cut with a single slot. It is always ink or white, never blue. The blue lives in the wordmark's square full stop.

Construction

Drawn on a 64-unit grid. Both counters are 8 units wide; the 0 wall is 7 units. The gap between the blocks is 4 units.

Misuse

The mark is solid and square. Anything that softens, tilts or decorates it breaks the idea.

Don't make the mark blue

Blue belongs to the full stop and the app tile only.

Don't rotate or skew

The blocks always stand upright on the baseline.

Don't round the corners

Corners stay square at every size.

Don't outline

The mark is always a solid fill, never a stroke.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings.
  • End display headlines with a full stop. One phrase may turn blue.
  • Set numbers with tabular figures so columns line up.
  • Use mono only for labels, data, code and step numbers, never for paragraphs.

Light lines, square logic.

Drawing rules

  • 24 × 24 grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps and round joins. Outline only.
  • Straight lines at 0°, 45° and 90°. Corners of shapes get a 1.5px radius.
  • Keep a 3px clear border inside the grid; match the optical weight of the arrow.
  • Active state: an ink tile with the blue corner square, the same device as the full stop.

Don't

  • Mix in filled or heavy icon sets. If you need a missing icon, draw it to these rules.
  • Use robots, brains, sparkles or magic wands for AI. Use the model grid.
  • Colour icons blue by default. Blue marks state, not decoration.
  • Use emoji as icons anywhere in the product or on the site.
05 · Motion

Mechanical, quick, then still.

Things move like well-made parts: they rise into place, open from a slot and stop. Motion confirms an action or reveals structure. The one thing that loops is the process flow in schematics, slowly, to show work moving. Press replay on each pattern.

06 · Imagery & texture

Drawn like a schematic.

Show the work as engineering drawings: real workflows mapped in hairline strokes on a faint grid, with square nodes, 45° turns and mono labels. Blue marks only the finished step. Photography, when used, is black and white with a fine grain.

Use

  • Main paths at 0.75px, grid and dashed manual steps at 0.5px. Strokes never scale with the drawing.
  • Right angles and 45° turns only, on a 20px grid. Nodes are 10px open squares.
  • Label every node in mono caps with its step number. Blue fills the finished step only.
  • Draw the client's real process. Dashed grey lines show the manual work we remove.
  • In motion, a blue unit with a short trail travels each main path at 160 units a second and lights each node it passes. Manual steps crawl in grey at a third of that speed.

Avoid

  • Pixel, dither or halftone effects, and robots, glowing brains or neon circuits.
  • Purple-to-blue gradients and glossy 3D renders.
  • Stock handshakes and staged meetings.
  • Client logos as decoration without a result attached.

08 · Voice

Numbers over adjectives.

Write like an engineer explaining the fix to an operations director: plain English, short sentences, the problem first and the result in numbers.

Write

We cut month-end close from 38 hours to 4.

Not

We revolutionise finance with cutting-edge AI.

Write

Your team still makes the decisions. The system does the copying.

Not

Seamless, intelligent automation for the enterprise of tomorrow.

Write

Book a diagnostic

Not

Unlock your AI journey today!

Say "we" and "you"

We are the team doing the work; you are the business with the problem.

Name the process

"Dispute handling", "order entry", "month-end close", not "operations".

Banned words

Revolutionise, cutting-edge, seamless, synergy, unlock, magic, next-gen.
